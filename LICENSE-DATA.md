## What the data contains

Branch identities (file name, line, block and branch number) of third-party programs, counts of branches, curves of
branch counts over time, counts of sanitiser findings and counts of model output tokens. It contains no source code,
test inputs, stack traces or personal data. The programs the branch identities refer to are distributed under their
own licences; the licence above applies to the measurements and to this compilation, not to those programs.
